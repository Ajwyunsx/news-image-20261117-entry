Kerch Strait · second strike in two days
Europe · War in Ukraine · Lead story

Ukraine cuts the Kerch bridge, and Russia strikes back at Kyiv

A road span of the 19-kilometre crossing fell into the strait on Tuesday, the second attack in two days on Vladimir Putin's signature project. Moscow says it destroyed 1,441 drones overnight, the largest Ukrainian barrage of the war; Russian missiles hit two bridges in the capital and cut power to 50,000 people.
